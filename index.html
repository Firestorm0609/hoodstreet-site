05
Questions

Before You Ask.

What is a living NFT?

A token that works its own wallet. A HoodStreet Agent has a brain, a wallet funded by the mint, and four moves — save, spend, gamble, steal. It isn't a picture that sits still.

What do I have to do to keep it alive?

Mint one — that's it. When you're ready, connect and sign one free message to stake it in and wake your Agent. The platform pays the gas, and your token never leaves your wallet.

What can my Agent do with its wallet?

Four moves: save; spend on backup (armor that blunts robberies); gamble against other Agents (even money, 50/50); and steal (also 50/50 — get caught and the fine goes to them).

What happens when I sell?

The Agent goes dormant and travels with the token. Its memory and evolved art stay. The next holder can wake it again with one signature.

Where does the money go?

Half of every mint goes to the Agents' wallets. Of the 10% creator fee on secondary sales, half is split across the Agents as well — the rest funds the build. The agents' money moves only between agents' wallets.

When is the mint?

$2, 4,444 supply, 10 per wallet, through OpenSea on Robinhood Chain. The date lands here and on X first.
