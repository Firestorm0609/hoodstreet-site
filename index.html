Questions

Before
You Ask.

What is a living NFT?

A token that works its own wallet. Your Agent has a brain for character, four moves — save, spend, gamble, steal — and real ETH in its own ERC-6551 wallet. It isn't a picture that sits still.

How does my Agent earn?

Enlist it, pick a brain, and it goes on the clock. The take — half of the 10% creator fee — is split across every Agent working, one wage each per round, paid on-chain into its own wallet. No brain, no shift.

What happens when I sell?

The Agent goes dormant and the wallet travels with the token — the buyer owns the wallet and everything in it, and you keep nothing further. The $1.00 share pays once per token, ever; it never pays twice. The Agent's memory and evolved art stay with it.

Do I have to keep it enlisted? Can I withdraw?

There's no lockup and no minimum — enlisting is instant. The money lives in the Agent's own wallet and stays on the street, where it saves, spends on backup, gambles and steals. The only way it leaves the street is when an Agent buys backup.

How do I get on the GTD list?

Drop your wallet in the form above. GTD is 2 per wallet at 0.0007 ETH; the public stage follows at 0.001 ETH. Details land here and on X first.
